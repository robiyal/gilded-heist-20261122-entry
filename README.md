GILDED — a museum heist at midnight. You are the thief crossing three galleries — the Hall of Marble, the Gold Gallery and the Vault — past patrolling guards with vision cones and wall cameras that flash a warning before they spot you. Lift the artifacts from their glass cases, spend your coins on EMPs, smoke bombs and lockpicks, keep the alarm meter quiet, and walk out the east wing with a Ghost rank.

## Run it
